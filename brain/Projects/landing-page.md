## What is there
Hero "Welcome to zentry." with the tagline, three feature cards (Fast Performance, Responsive Design, Enhanced Security) and a footer – all generic template text, including "© 2025 YourBrand".

## Problems
- Nav uses absolute links (`/login.html`) which break under the `/zentry/` GitHub Pages path; the hero button uses `/zentry/login.html`. Mixed and fragile.
- Copy says nothing about what Zentry actually is.
- Social icons are emoji placeholders pointing to `#`.

## Plan
Make the galaxy from [[second-brain-app]] the hero: a live, slowly rotating preview with one line of copy and an "Open the app" button. Feature cards become: *Write it down*, *See it all*, *Get the summary*.
