## Decision
Each group owns a direction around the black hole. Every idea is a star in its group's cloud; recently updated pages sit closer to the centre, old ones drift outward.

## Why
The reference demo was beautiful but the positions meant nothing. Here a glance answers "what have I been working on lately?" – see [[second-brain-app]].

## Details
- Brighter, bigger stars = ideas from pages updated recently.
- Lines = `[[links]]` between pages.
- Pulsing star = page with an open task.
