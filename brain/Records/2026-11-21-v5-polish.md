## Done
- **Gravitational lensing**: the accretion disk's far side now bends over the top of the black hole and its near side under the bottom, made of the same spinning particles.
- **Spiral arms**: the galaxy's dust follows two logarithmic arms that turn slowly.
- **Blooming pages**: opening a page fans its sections out into a labelled ring around it; tapping a section scrolls the reader there.
- **Home comes alive**: cards rise in, counters count up, bars and sparklines grow; the greeting lines stagger in.
- **Shortcuts sheet** on `?` (and the ? button), light haptic tap on Android.
