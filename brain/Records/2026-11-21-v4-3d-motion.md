## Done
- Rebuilt the renderer in real 3D with perspective: groups, pages and dust have depth, the cloud turns slowly on its own and sways, dragging rotates it with inertia, two fingers / shift-drag pan.
- Comets fly on tilted 3D orbits; shooting stars cross the sky now and then.
- Opening animation: the galaxy swings in from a tilted, far view while stars fade in one by one and the black hole flares.
- Clicking the black hole dives into it and spins the accretion disk up before Home opens.
- Opening a group sends ripples through it; the view settles face-on while you read; hovered stars get a spinning ring.
