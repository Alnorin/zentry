## Asked
Shared a screen recording of a "Second Brain" app – a galaxy of notes around a black hole with a Home dashboard – and asked for something like it, only better: a second brain where everything done is written down and summarised, plus many other features.

## Done
Built v1 `brain.html`: colourful galaxy (angle = area, distance = recency), quick capture bar with `#group`, `!`, `@date`, auto summaries for day/week/month, tasks, search, focus mode, JSON export. Data stored in the browser.

## Outcome
Rejected – see [[first-prototype-review]] and the follow-up [[2026-11-21-v2-rewrite]].
