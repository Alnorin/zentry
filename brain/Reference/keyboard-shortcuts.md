## Navigation
- `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` – search every page
- `H` – Home dashboard
- `P` – Pages list
- `Esc` – close / back to the galaxy

## Galaxy
- Scroll or pinch – zoom
- Drag – pan
- `Space` – pause the motion
- `0` – reset view
